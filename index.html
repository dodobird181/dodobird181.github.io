Hi, I'm Sabrina

I'm a florist based in Montréal, and I've loved flowers for as long as I can remember. What started as picking bouquets for friends and family has grown into arranging flowers for dinners, celebrations, and everything in between.

I like arrangements that feel fresh and natural, with a nice variety of greenery to accompany the flowers themselves. My approach is different for each person, and every piece I make is carefully put together by hand.
